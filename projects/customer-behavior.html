Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

Turning thousands of purchase records into clear strategies for customer retention and revenue growth.

Executive Summary

A retail business had thousands of customer transactions but no clear understanding of what drives repeat purchases or which customer groups bring the most value. I cleaned, analyzed, and visualized the data using Python, SQL, and Power BI to uncover behavioral patterns. The analysis revealed key insights about the impact of discounts, subscription programs, and age demographics on revenue — leading to 5 actionable business strategies.

Domain Retail / E-Commerce
Role Data Analyst
Stack Python / SQL / Power BI
Dataset 3,900 Transactions
01 / The Business Problem

Data Rich, Insight Poor

Who Are Our Best Customers?

The business couldn't identify which age groups, genders, or customer segments were generating the most revenue. Marketing was generic, not targeted.

Are Discounts Actually Working?

Discounts were being offered without data to prove whether they actually increased customer spending or just cut into profit margins.

Why Don't Customers Come Back?

There was no segmentation to separate new, returning, and loyal customers. Retention programs were based on guesswork, not data.

02 / My Analysis Process

From Raw Data to Clear Answers

A structured 3-step approach to transform messy transaction data into actionable business intelligence.

Step 01
🧹

Data Cleaning (Python)

Used Pandas to clean 3,900 records: handled missing values, standardized data types, created age groups, and prepared the dataset for analysis. Exported a clean CSV ready for SQL and Power BI.

Python + Pandas
Step 02
🔍

Deep SQL Analysis

Wrote 10 advanced SQL queries using CTEs, CASE statements, window functions (ROW_NUMBER), subqueries, and aggregations to answer critical business questions about revenue, segmentation, and product performance.

PostgreSQL / SQL Server
Step 03
📊

Power BI Dashboard

Built an interactive dashboard to visualize the findings: customer segmentation breakdown, revenue by age group, discount impact analysis, and product category performance with dynamic filters.

Power BI
03 / What The Data Revealed

Key Business Insights

10 SQL queries answered the most important questions about customer behavior and revenue drivers.

01

Revenue by Gender

Identified the total revenue split between male and female customers, revealing whether marketing should be gender-targeted or universal.

02

Smart Discount Shoppers

Found customers who used discounts but still spent above the average purchase amount — these are high-value customers worth retaining with loyalty perks.

03

Top-Rated Products

Ranked the top 5 products by average review rating, helping the business know which items to feature prominently in marketing campaigns.

04

Shipping & Spending Correlation

Compared average spend between Standard vs. Express shipping users. Customers choosing Express shipping tend to spend more per order.

05

Subscription Impact

Compared subscribers vs. non-subscribers on average spend and total revenue. This data directly answers: "Is our subscription program worth it?"

06

Customer Segmentation

Used a CTE to classify all customers into New, Returning, and Loyal segments based on their purchase history, giving the business a clear view of their customer base composition.

07

Revenue by Age Group

Broke down total revenue contribution by age group to identify the most profitable demographic — critical for ad targeting and product positioning.

📈

Top Products per Category

Used ROW_NUMBER() window function to rank the top 3 best-selling products within each category — providing category managers with clear bestseller data.

04 / Actionable Strategies

5 Business Recommendations

Data-backed strategies the business can implement immediately to improve retention and revenue.

🎯

1. Launch a Loyalty Program for "Smart Discount" Shoppers

Customers who use discounts but still spend above average are the most valuable segment. Offer them exclusive membership perks instead of generic percentage-off coupons.

👥

2. Target the Highest-Revenue Age Group

Focus marketing spend on the age demographic that generates the most revenue. Tailor product recommendations and ad creatives to resonate with this group.

🔄

3. Convert "Returning" Customers to "Loyal"

The segmentation reveals a large pool of Returning customers (1-10 purchases). Create re-engagement email campaigns with personalized product picks to push them into the Loyal tier.

📦

4. Promote Express Shipping as a Premium Upsell

Since Express shipping users spend more, bundle it as a "free upgrade" for orders above a certain threshold to encourage larger cart sizes.

⭐

5. Feature Top-Rated Products in Marketing

Use the top 5 highest-rated products as the face of campaigns. Social proof (high ratings) combined with visibility drives stronger conversion rates.

05 / Professional Takeaway

Data Without Action is Just Numbers

This project demonstrates the full cycle of a Data Analyst's value: taking raw, unstructured transaction data and transforming it into business strategies that a non-technical stakeholder can act on immediately. The combination of Python for data preparation, SQL for deep querying, and Power BI for visual storytelling ensures that the insights reach the right people in the right format.
