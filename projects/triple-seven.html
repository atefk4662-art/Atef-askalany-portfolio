02 / Problem Statement

The Operational Challenge

Securing operations, preventing cash leakage, and digitizing manual management.

Cash & Shift Discrepancies

Gaming lounges operate across continuous shifts. Without strict automated tracking, manual handover processes lead to discrepancies between recorded revenue and expected drawer cash.

Inventory Leakage

Cafeteria and inventory items are high-turnover assets. Manual tracking fails to provide real-time alerts when stock reaches critical lows, risking lost sales or unrecorded consumption.

Lack of Executive Visibility

Owners needed a way to track the overall ROI, monitor daily profitability (P&L), and manage the business remotely without needing to be physically present at the location.

04 / Product Modules

The Triple Seven Interface

A zero-template, custom-engineered interface designed for rapid decision-making.

Central Command Center

The primary system interface provides top management with a real-time overview of the business status. It acts as a radar summarizing daily performance, active shifts, and highlighting urgent system alerts (e.g., Critical Low Stock) to facilitate rapid decision-making.

Executive Dashboard

  • At-a-Glance Status: Instant view of the current shift and active staff.
  • Critical Alerts: Top-level display of depleted inventory items requiring immediate refill.

Master Ledger & Revenue Pulse

A precise financial analysis tool that documents gross income, tracks operational expenses (internet, rent, utilities), and automatically calculates net surpluses. It tracks capital growth and the percentage of initial capital recovery to ensure long-term stability.

Financial Transparency

  • ROI Tracking: Live percentage of capital recovered vs. total asset value.
  • Expense Ledger: Detailed outflow tracking to prevent unseen financial drains.

Operational Handover Governance

Live monitoring of the current work shift. The system tracks shift revenues continuously and strictly calculates the expected physical cash in the drawer, locking down the numbers during handover to prevent discrepancies.

Shift Guard

  • Expected Drawer Cash: Mathematical certainty of what must be in the register.
  • Blind Handovers: Enforced accountability between shift changes.

Smart Stock Intelligence

Precise tracking of all cafeteria movements. It acts as an early warning system, automatically alerting management when inventory reaches critical low levels, ensuring product availability while preventing unrecorded leakage.

Inventory Control

  • Unit Cost vs. Selling Price: Automated profit margin tracking per item.
  • Auto-Alerts: Zero-downtime notifications for out-of-stock risks.
05 / The Architect

Technical Proof of Work

Behind the code: A 100% custom-engineered architecture.

Zero Templates 💻

Custom JS Engine

Built a proprietary engine (`engine_v79.js`) from scratch to dynamically process and render P&L charts without relying on heavy third-party dashboard templates.

Data Integrity ⚙️

Backend Logic & Cache Busting

Developed `pulse.php` to govern Live Shift revenue in real-time, accompanied by advanced Cache Busting mechanisms to ensure absolutely zero stale data on management screens.

Military-Grade Security 🛡️

Encrypted Tailnet Routing

To provide secure remote access ("Project in your pocket"), the system utilizes Tailscale VPN technology, ensuring that the database and executive dashboards are completely invisible to the public internet while remaining instantly accessible to ownership from anywhere in the world.
